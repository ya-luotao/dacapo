An instructional illustration of good posture at the piano. A young adult learner sits on a piano bench at an upright piano, seen from behind and a little to the right, so the whole keyboard is visible from its left end to its right end. The learner sits exactly at the centre of the keyboard, with the back upright but relaxed, shoulders loose, forearms level with the keys, elbows slightly in front of the body, feet flat on the floor, and both hands resting lightly on the keys in the middle of the keyboard. The bench is at a sensible distance, so the elbows are neither cramped nor stretched. The keyboard is drawn correctly: white keys, and black keys in alternating groups of two and three.

Style: a copperplate etching, like the plates in a 19th-century piano method book. Fine black-brown ink line work with engraved hatching and cross-hatching for shading; no colour other than dark brown-black ink (#231d18) on warm cream paper (#f8f4ec). Calm, precise, instructional, elegant. Generous plain paper around the subject. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, musical notation, signature, watermark, frame or border.
