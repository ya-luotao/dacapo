An instructional illustration of good posture at the piano, seen exactly from the side (a profile view). A young adult sits on a piano bench at an upright piano. The bench is at a height where the forearms are level with the keyboard, the elbows a little in front of the body and slightly higher than the keys, the back upright and leaning very slightly forward from the hips, shoulders relaxed and low, knees just under the front edge of the keyboard, both feet flat on the floor, the right foot near the pedals. The hands rest on the keys with gently curved fingers. The upright piano is seen from its end, so only the edge of the keyboard shows.

Style: a copperplate etching, like the plates in a 19th-century piano method book. Fine black-brown ink line work with engraved hatching and cross-hatching for shading; no colour other than dark brown-black ink (#231d18) on warm cream paper (#f8f4ec). Calm, precise, instructional, elegant. Generous plain paper around the subject. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, musical notation, signature, watermark, frame or border.
