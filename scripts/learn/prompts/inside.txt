An illustration of the inside of a grand piano with its lid raised, seen from above and a little to the side, looking along the instrument from the curved tail towards the keyboard. The cast-iron frame with its round openings, the long strings fanning out over the soundboard and crossing the wooden bridge, the rows of tuning pins near the front, and the dampers resting on the strings, with the row of felt hammers glimpsed beneath the strings near the keyboard end. The keyboard itself is far away and mostly hidden by the frame. A sense of precise, beautiful machinery.
